# Deterministic simulation core

Implemented on 2026-10-26. All distances are SI metres, masses kilograms, time seconds, orbital angles radians. Coordinates use Unity Y up.

## Decisions and sources

- **Coordinates:** integer `long` sectors of 1e12 m and double local offsets in [-5e11, 5e11). Local precision is at worst approximately 0.061 mm before relative subtraction. Sector differences are computed exactly before double conversion, including opposite Int64 extremes. Local precision does not degrade as the absolute sector increases. Converting a whole galactic position into one double is deliberately avoided. An object at astronomical distance from the observer necessarily has lower relative double precision; origin rebasing only promises precision near the observer.
- **Two-body orbits:** the orbital-plane ellipse and element rotation follow the [JPL Solar System Dynamics Keplerian element description](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/planets/approx_pos.html). The solver uses safeguarded Newton iteration with a bisection bracket rather than assuming convergence for high eccentricity. Time is reduced modulo orbital period first. Planet mass is included in the two-body gravitational parameter. These are independent ideal two-body ellipses; mutual perturbations, relativistic corrections and real-catalogue ephemerides are outside this implementation.
- **Randomness:** [Sebastiano Vigna's public-domain SplitMix64 reference](https://prng.di.unimi.it/splitmix64.c) supplies the stream's mixing constants and algorithm. All 64 bits of each signed cell coordinate enter the hash; Unity global random state and process-dependent string hashes are unused. `GenerationVersion = 1` records the catalogue algorithm version; maintaining seed compatibility requires keeping that version's implementation.
- **Terrain:** the gradient-noise basis and quintic interpolation derive from [Ken Perlin, Improving Noise, SIGGRAPH 2002, author reference](https://cs.nyu.edu/~perlin/noise/) and the author's [GPU Gems explanation](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems/part-i-natural-effects/chapter-5-implementing-improved-perlin-noise). This is an original hash-gradient implementation, without the reference permutation table. Sampling normalized directions in 3D avoids longitude seams and pole singularities. Independent continental, hill, ridged mountain and detail bands combine into smooth bounded heights; a tanh envelope guarantees `abs(height) <= TerrainHeight`. The sea datum is zero, and seabed remains present under ocean rendering.
- **Population:** jittered cells are four light years across, providing locally plausible spacing without storing every star. This version generates 4-7 separated terrestrial or super-Earth planets per system. It is an exploration population model, not a calibrated galaxy distribution or planet-formation simulation. The origin cell is placed exactly at the origin for stable startup. Its first planet always has atmosphere and ocean, while every physical parameter, terrain and orbital phase still depends on the universe seed.

## Automated acceptance

`Assets/SpaceEngine/Tests/EditMode` covers millimetre motion at galactic and Int64 sector positions, both boundary signs, overflow rejection, 64-bit cell sensitivity, repeatable complete generated definitions, physically separated orbits across 32 systems, Kepler residuals through e=0.999999, year-scale period, ellipse extrema, inclination and negative time, seeded terrain, seam/pole continuity, and 1024 spherical terrain samples for variation, sea/land and conservative bounds. These numerical tests do not validate graphics; rendered terrain needs separate screenshot and runtime verification.
